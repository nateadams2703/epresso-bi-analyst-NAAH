**Status:** Part 1 (the modified row) and Part 2 (predictions) were written and pushed **before** the
upload, Oct 29, 2026. Parts 3–4 and the summary are filled in after the upload.

up by exactly $1,000, the file is uploaded through the app with no code changes and no restart,
and we compare what happened with what we predicted.
